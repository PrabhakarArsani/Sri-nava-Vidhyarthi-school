About Our School

Discover the story behind Sri Nava Vidhyarthi School

Our Story

Sri Nava Vidhyarthi School was established with a vision to provide quality education that nurtures the holistic development of every child. Located in the heart of YSR Colony, Almasguda, our school has been serving the community with dedication and excellence.

We believe that every child is unique and has unlimited potential waiting to be discovered. Our school provides a nurturing environment where students can explore, learn, and grow into confident, responsible, and compassionate individuals.

Our Mission

To provide comprehensive education that fosters academic excellence, character development, and creative expression, preparing students to become responsible global citizens who contribute positively to society.

Our Vision

To be a leading educational institution that inspires lifelong learning, promotes innovation, and cultivates values that shape the leaders of tomorrow.

Our Core Values

Excellence

We strive for excellence in everything we do, encouraging students to reach their full potential.

Compassion

We foster empathy and kindness, teaching students to care for others and the world around them.

Integrity

We uphold the highest standards of honesty, ethics, and moral character in all our actions.

Collaboration

We believe in the power of teamwork and cooperation to achieve common goals.
