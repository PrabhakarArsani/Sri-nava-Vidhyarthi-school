Our Facilities

School facilities are the physical infrastructure and resources provided to support the educational, recreational, and administrative needs of students, teachers, and staff.

Modern Classrooms

Equipped with desks, chairs, whiteboards, and smartboards for interactive learning experiences.

Library

Spaces for reading, studying, and accessing books, e-resources, and other reference materials.

For outdoor activities and sports like football, basketball, and track events.
