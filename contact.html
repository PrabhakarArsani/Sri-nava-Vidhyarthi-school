Frequently Asked Questions

What are the admission requirements?

Admission requirements include age verification, previous school records, and a simple interaction session. We welcome students from all backgrounds and provide equal opportunities for quality education.

What is the fee structure?

Our fee structure is competitive and transparent. It includes tuition fees, activity charges, and other necessary expenses. We also offer scholarships for deserving students.

What extracurricular activities do you offer?

We offer a wide range of extracurricular activities including sports, music, dance, art, science clubs, and cultural programs to ensure holistic development of students.

What are the school timings?

School timings are from 8:00 AM to 3:30 PM on weekdays and 8:00 AM to 1:00 PM on Saturdays. We also offer extended care programs for working parents.
